# Embedded Mastery — Embedded Systems Programming (CCS4207) Mock Exam & Section Portal

> **Course:** CCS4207 — Embedded Systems Programming  
> **Institution:** College of Computing & Information Technology, Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport (AASTMT)  
> **Instructor:** Dr. Ehab Elsehely  
> **Source Material:** Section Sheets 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 & Official Model Answers  
> **Coverage:** Weeks 01 through 06 (System Definitions, 6 Purposes, DSP/Harvard/Endianness, Logic Optimization & Adders, ARM Cortex-M & TM4C123 Programming)

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Section Sheet & Module Selector:**
   - **All Sheets Comprehensive Exam:** Full 90-minute simulated examination combining questions across the whole syllabus.
   - **Sheet 1 (Week 1):** Introduction to Embedded Systems, Microprocessors vs Microcontrollers, AGC History & Components.
   - **Sheet 2 (Week 2):** The 6 Primary Purposes, Hard vs Soft Real-Time Systems, ASIPs & Reliability.
   - **Sheet 3 (Week 3):** Processors, DSPs, MAC units, Circular Buffers, RISC vs CISC, Harvard vs Von-Neumann & Endianness.
   - **Sheet 4 (Week 4):** Digital Circuit Design, Boolean Algebra Reduction, K-Maps, Half/Full Adders & Pin Multiplexing.
   - **Sheet 5 (Week 6):** ARM Cortex-A/R/M Profiles, TM4C123GH6PM Hardware, Run-Mode Clock Gating, Bit-Banding & GPIO Masking.

2. **Dual Learning Modes:**
   - **Timed Exam Simulation Mode:** Configurable examination countdown with unattempted warnings, question bookmarking, auto-submit on timeout, and celebration effects.
   - **Interactive Practice & Study Mode:** Instant feedback upon clicking any option, displaying detailed pedagogical explanations, official model answers, and verbatim lecture quotes.

3. **Self-Scoring Rubrics for Problem Solving:**
   - Every short essay, logic reduction, and code implementation question includes the official model answer and step-by-step scoring rubric.

4. **Class Scoreboard & Leaderboard:**
   - Real-time ranking with medal badges (🥇 #1, 🥈 #2, 🥉 #3), score breakdown, percentage, and elapsed time tracking.
   - Integrated with local storage and cloud sync.

5. **Official Reference Answer Key & Printable Cheat Sheet:**
   - Searchable, categorized repository of all 125+ questions with official answers.
   - Print-optimized CSS for generating clean offline revision notes.
